      Frequently Asked Questions

      Where do the replacement-camera figures come from?

      From you. Camera and installation cost varies enormously by model, mounting, cable run and region, so putting a single number in would be misleading. The default is a mid-range placeholder for a professional IP camera supplied and installed — change it to whatever your own quotes say and the comparison becomes meaningful.

      Why is the PARVEK camera cost zero?

      Because the premise of the comparison is that your existing cameras qualify and are retained. Where a location genuinely cannot support detection, that camera becomes a line item — add those to the "cameras still needing replacement" field and the calculator accounts for them.

      Does this include setup?

      Setup is a field you control, because it depends on the site assessment — camera count, network work, mounting and configuration. Leave it at zero to compare software and hardware alone, or enter your expected figure for a fuller picture.

      Is this a quotation?

      No. It is arithmetic on list prices and your own assumptions. A real number comes after a site assessment, which is also when we find the things a calculator cannot — the camera pointed at a wall, the link that will not carry the traffic, the area that needs a light before it needs AI.

      AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.
