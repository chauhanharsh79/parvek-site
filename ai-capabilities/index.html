Capabilities

Capability reference

Each capability states what it does, where it helps, a real example and the response that can be configured for it. Not every capability is enabled on every deployment.

Detect

Identify people, vehicles, objects and security events as they happen.
Person Detection +
Detects
A person in the camera view, separated from the movement that is not a person.
Where it helps
Perimeters, entrances, yards, corridors and any area that should be empty at certain hours.
Example
Someone crosses a boundary wall at 2:50 AM and walks toward the building.
Configurable response
Alert with the frame and clip · voice warning, light or siren where configured.
Vehicle Detection +
Detects
Vehicles entering, leaving or present in an area, including crossings of a line you draw.
Where it helps
Gates, parking, loading areas, service entries and yards.
Example
A vehicle enters the yard at an hour with no scheduled movement.
Configurable response
Timestamped frame per crossing · in/out counts · gate movement report.
Fire & Smoke Detection +
Detects
Visible flame and smoke in the camera view.
Where it helps
Plant rooms, stores, kitchens, parking, high bays and open yards where heat takes time to reach a detector.
Example
A fire starts in a store room overnight with nobody on that floor.
Configurable response
Critical alert with clip · maintenance notified · horn where configured.
Fall Detection +
Detects
A person collapsing or lying on the floor, from skeleton-based analysis.
Where it helps
Stairwells, basements, wards, plant rooms and night shifts where nobody may be watching.
Example
A lone operator collapses in a plant room during a night shift.
Configurable response
Immediate alert with clip · voice announcement where configured.
Occupancy & Crowd Monitoring +
Detects
How many people are in an area, and when that exceeds the limit you set.
Where it helps
Gates, lobbies, canteens, waiting areas, work bays and meeting spaces.
Example
A gathering builds at the main gate during a shift change.
Configurable response
Alert with the live count and clip · occupancy trend per area and shift.
Footfall Counting & Dwell Time +
Detects
How many people enter and leave across a line, and how long they stay in an area once inside.
Where it helps
Store entrances, aisles and displays, showroom floors, service counters, waiting areas and lobbies.
Example
Footfall at the entrance holds steady through the afternoon while dwell at one display drops away — the count says traffic is fine, the dwell says the display stopped working.
Configurable response
Entry and exit counts per hour and per day · dwell time per area · trend by day of week, comparable across sites.
Intrusion & Restricted-Area Detection +
Detects
A person inside an area you have marked, during the hours it is off limits.
Where it helps
Plant rooms, stock racks, cash offices, server rooms, docks and perimeters.
Example
A person stands at the stock racks after the permitted window has closed.
Configurable response
Local voice warning · light in that area · silent alert and clip to the desk.

Understand

Add identity, context, movement and event intelligence to live video.
Face Recognition +
Detects
Enrolled people, so the staff and contractors who belong there are known rather than flagged.
Where it helps
Main gate, reception, lobby and floor entrances.
Example
A regular contractor arrives at 9 AM and is recognized instead of raising an intruder alert.
Configurable response
Named event · silent log entry · notification where you want one.
Person Re-Identification +
Detects
The same person across cameras and over time, without relying on a clear face in every frame.
Where it helps
Multi-camera sites where someone moves between areas — corridors, yards, floors.
Example
A visitor enters at reception and is followed through to a restricted corridor as one event, not five.
Configurable response
Events linked into a single visit · the path through the site available as evidence.
Vehicle Identification +
Detects
Vehicle attributes — type, and where the camera supports it, distinguishing characteristics.
Where it helps
Gates, weighbridges, parking and dispatch.
Example
A truck returns to the dock twice in a shift when the paperwork shows one visit.
Configurable response
Vehicle events with the frame · gate log for review.
License Plate Recognition +
Detects
The number plate of a vehicle, read and logged against the crossing.
Where it helps
Gates, parking entries and dispatch, where the camera, lens, angle and lighting suit plate capture.
Example
A vehicle enters at night and you need the plate, not just a count and a frame.
Configurable response
Plate logged with the entry · searchable gate log · alert on a listed plate where configured.
Behavior & Context Analysis +
Detects
How a person is moving — lingering, pacing, approaching a boundary — rather than only that they are present.
Where it helps
Perimeters, racks, ATMs, entrances and quiet corridors where presence alone is not the signal.
Example
Someone waits beside the boundary for several minutes without approaching the gate.
Configurable response
Escalating alert once your time threshold is met · voice warning · evidence clip.
Identity-Aware Event Intelligence +
Detects
Who an event involves, not just that it happened — including matches against a watchlist you maintain.
Where it helps
Entrances, visitor areas and any zone with a named list of who may enter.
Example
A barred former contractor returns to the site and is matched at the gate.
Configurable response
Alert with the matched photo so the desk confirms before acting · the list stays on your appliance.

Act

Trigger alerts, sirens, speakers, lights, guard workflows and evidence when action matters.
Real-Time Alerts +
Detects
Every configured event, delivered while it is still happening.
Where it helps
The security desk, the operations console and the mobile app.
Example
A restricted-area breach reaches the desk in seconds, with the frame that triggered it.
Configurable response
Alert with snapshot and clip · acknowledge, resolve or escalate from the console.
Siren & Speaker Integration +
Detects
Not a detection — the response. A voice warning or audible alarm driven by the event.
Where it helps
The specific area that was breached, rather than across the whole site.
Example
The speaker in that room challenges the person while they are still standing there.
Configurable response
Local voice warning · recorded or live talk-down · audible escalation where the site permits it.
Floodlight & Response Integration +
Detects
Not a detection — lights and other devices switched by the appliance when a rule matches.
Where it helps
Perimeters, yards, driveways and any dark area where light is both deterrent and evidence.
Example
The area lights up as someone crosses the boundary, and the camera gets a usable image.
Configurable response
Light or floodlight on · relay-driven devices switched · all logged against the event.
Guard Workflows +
Detects
Patrol checkpoints confirmed by the guard on each round, and attendance at the post.
Where it helps
Checkpoint cameras along the patrol route, security desks and reception posts.
Example
A checkpoint is missed on the 3 AM round, or a desk is left unattended beyond the allowed window.
Configurable response
Supervisor alert · patrol log of who was where and when · occupancy report per shift.
Mobile & Dashboard Notifications +
Detects
Not a detection — delivery. Events pushed to the people who need them, wherever they are.
Where it helps
Phones for the owner, supervisor or on-call responder; the dashboard for the desk.
Example
A critical event reaches three people at once while the site is empty.
Configurable response
Push notification with the frame · live view · SOS and panic raised from the app.
Evidence Clips & Reporting +
Detects
Not a detection — the record. Every event connected to the footage behind it.
Where it helps
Across every camera and capability in use.
Example
A month-end review, or an incident that has to be reconstructed exactly.
Configurable response
Frame, clip, time and context per event · playback and export · reports kept on the appliance.
Restricted Zones & Access

A camera records the room. A zone watches it.

Your store rooms, server or records room, plant rooms and entrances are already on camera. What is missing is a rule: which area, during which hours, and who is allowed in it. PARVEK marks each one as a zone and alerts the security desk the moment it is broken — not three weeks later when someone reviews the footage.

Rule part 01 Area

A polygon drawn on the camera view — the racks or the cabinet, not the whole frame.

+
Rule part 02 Permitted hours

Working hours, night only, or always.

+
Rule part 03 Who is allowed

Enrolled staff only, a named list, or nobody after hours.

=
Result Named alert

Security desk, in seconds, with the clip — and the area can answer back with voice and light.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all of these available on every deployment?

No, and that is deliberate. Capabilities are selected per site during the assessment and configured at commissioning — a home runs a handful, a plant may use all three groups. Several depend on camera placement, lens and lighting to work well, which is part of what the assessment establishes.

Can we add capabilities later?

Yes. Once the cameras and the appliance are in, additional capabilities are a configuration change rather than new hardware — subject to the cameras covering the right areas well enough.

How accurate is the detection?

Accuracy depends on the camera, the lighting, the angle and the scene. That is why deployments start with an assessment and end with tuning on your own footage rather than a benchmark. AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.

Does face recognition mean our data goes somewhere?

No. Enrolled faces, clips and reports are held on the appliance inside your premises, and users and roles control who can see what. Biometric regulation varies by jurisdiction, so confirm your obligations with counsel — the system is useful with recognition disabled, using areas, timing and person detection alone.

What triggers a physical response rather than just an alert?

You decide, per area. A common configuration is a local voice warning and light in the area that was breached, with a silent alert and clip to the desk — keeping sirens and floodlights for the perimeter and only where the site permits them.

AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.

Which of these does your site need?

Schedule a site assessment. We walk the premises, mark the areas that need rules with your team, and recommend the capabilities worth enabling — and the ones that are not.
