What We See Here

Security on Tracy sites

Tracy buildings are newer and better covered than most, which changes the problem: the cameras are already decent and already recording, and still nobody watches them until something has gone missing. Here the gap is attention rather than coverage, and that is a software problem.

What Comes Up

The problems these sites actually have

Dock doors outside scheduled hours

Movement at a dock when nothing is booked. The clip comes attached to the alert, so a supervisor judges it in seconds rather than scrubbing an hour of timeline.

Trailer yards too large to watch

Vehicles entering, leaving and moving between slots, counted with a timestamped frame per crossing so gate activity can be checked against your own records.

High-value cages inside the building

Marked as restricted areas with permitted hours and who may be in them, rather than relying on a camera nobody reviews.

Shift-change crowding at gates

Headcount limits where crowding is a safety and throughput problem, with the live count going to the desk.

Frequently Asked Questions

We already have good camera coverage. What does PARVEK actually add?

Attention. Good coverage recording to a system nobody looks at still means incidents are found afterwards rather than as they happen. PARVEK watches those same streams and raises the specific things you asked for — a person in a cage after hours, a vehicle at a dock with nothing booked — with the frame and clip attached.

Do you have an office in Tracy?

No. Our US office is in Minneapolis, MN, and we deploy into California from there — we would rather say that than imply a local address we do not have. The assessment is done remotely from your camera models and views, and commissioning is scheduled on site.

Do we have to replace our cameras?

Usually not. PARVEK runs on supported cameras you already own, reading standard RTSP and ONVIF streams. The assessment confirms which of yours can carry detection and says plainly which cannot.

Where is the video processed?

On an appliance at your Tracy site. No continuous video leaves the premises, no inbound ports are opened, and the data stays on hardware you control.

How do we try it before committing?

A 30-day trial on your own cameras. The appliance is lent at no cost and collected if you do not continue, so you are not buying hardware to evaluate software.

AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.
