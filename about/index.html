About

From Parvekshak to PARVEK

Born as Parvekshak. Built for the world as PARVEK.

Start a Site Assessment I already have cameras

The platform began as Parvekshak — inspired by the Sanskrit-derived word Paryavekshaka (पर्यवेक्षक), meaning an observer, overseer, or one who watches closely.

It has been deployed on customer sites since 2025, watching plants, warehouses, gates and corridors.

PARVEK is the shorter global identity for the same platform — designed to make intelligent video security easier to say, remember and use worldwide.

PARVEKSHAK remains the brand in India, at parvekshak.com.

PARVEK Proactive AI for Real-Time Video Security Same platform. Local AI. Real-time understanding. Actionable evidence.
What PARVEK Stands For

Six principles define how PARVEK approaches video security

PProactive
The system acts while something is happening, not after someone reviews the footage.
AAI
Detection and intelligence models run locally on the appliance and are tuned for your cameras.
RReal-time
Seconds, not a nightly batch. The response can begin while the event is still happening.
VVideo
The cameras you already own, whatever the brand, wherever they point.
EEvent Intelligence
Not raw footage — the specific events that matter, with the evidence attached.
KKnowledge
Evidence, reports and context that stay on site — ready to be acted on, audited and trusted.
What We Build

One appliance, 18 capabilities

A single on-premise appliance that ingests your camera streams, records them, runs detection locally, raises events with the evidence attached, and drives the speakers, lights and workflows that let a site respond rather than only report.

It is delivered as a complete deployment — assessment, coverage design, configuration, commissioning, training and ongoing service — because a detection model handed over without those is not a security system.
