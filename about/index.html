The Name

From Parvekshak to PARVEK

The platform began as Parvekshak — from the Sanskrit परीक्षक, meaning the observer, the one who examines. It has been deployed on customer sites since 2025, watching plants, warehouses, gates and corridors.

PARVEK is the same platform under a shorter name for this market — and the letters were too good to leave alone:

What We Build

One appliance, 24 capabilities

A single on-premise appliance that ingests your camera streams, records them, runs detection locally, raises alerts with evidence attached, and drives the speakers, lights and horns that make a site respond on its own.

It is sold as a complete deployment — assessment, coverage design, configuration, commissioning, training and ongoing service — because a detection model handed over without those is not a security system.

How We Work

Four things we hold to

Detection is the product

A dashboard that reports what already happened is a recording system with extra steps. Everything here is built around detecting the event and doing something about it while it matters.

Your video stays yours

Processing runs on the appliance inside your building. Faces, clips and reports do not leave the premises, and there is no cloud subscription the AI depends on. If your connection drops, detection keeps running.

We would rather say no

Some cameras cannot carry detection, and some sites need a floodlight rather than a setting. We say so at assessment. Overclaiming wins one deal and loses the reference.

Tuned on your footage, not a datasheet

Accuracy depends on the camera, lighting, angle and scene. Every deployment ends with tuning against what your cameras actually see, across real days and nights.
