Frequently Asked Questions

How long is it?

Thirty to forty-five minutes, most of it driven by what you ask. If you send camera models or a screenshot of a view beforehand, we will aim the walkthrough at your site rather than a generic tour.

Do you need access to our cameras?

No. A demo runs on our own footage and console. Running it against your cameras is the 30-day trial, which is a separate and later step.

Can you demo Access Intelligence?

Not as a working product. Access Intelligence is being built now and is not yet deployed on customer sites. We are looking for design partners who run a PACS and want to shape it. What we can do is walk you through the design, show you where it fits against your PACS, and be straight about what is and is not built.

Will this turn into a sales sequence?

No. One person replies, you get the walkthrough, and if it is not a fit we say so. You will not be enrolled in a drip campaign because you asked for a demo.
