30-Day Free Trial

Try it on your own cameras, with the appliance on loan

Thirty days on your own site, your own cameras and your own footage. We lend the appliance at no cost, commission it with your team, and collect it if you decide not to continue.

Start with a site assessment Check your cameras first
No hardware purchase to evaluate.

PARVEK runs on an appliance on your premises, so a real trial needs one there. We lend it for the 30 days. You are evaluating detection on your own site — not buying a box to find out whether the software is any good.

What the Trial Includes

What you get for the 30 days

The appliance, lent at no cost

We provide the PARVEK appliance for the trial. You do not buy hardware to evaluate the software. If you decide not to continue, we arrange collection and that is the end of it.

Your cameras, not ours

The trial runs on the cameras already on your wall, so what you are judging is detection on your own scenes, lighting and angles — not a vendor demo on footage chosen to flatter the model.

Installation and configuration with you

We commission it alongside your team: camera connections, the zones that matter, the hours they matter, and who gets alerted. This is the work that decides whether a deployment is any good.

The capabilities your site needs

Which ones run is decided at the assessment rather than fixed by a plan. Capabilities that need more validation, such as face recognition or specialized models, are agreed before the trial starts.

Tuning on your own footage

No site is right on day one. We review what fired and what did not during the trial and adjust thresholds, zones and schedules against your real events.

A written read at the end

What worked, what did not, which cameras are holding you back, and what a full deployment would involve. You keep that whether or not you proceed.

What We Need From You

What makes a trial worth running

A trial is only useful if it tests the real thing. These are the conditions that make it a fair test rather than a demo.

01
A site assessment first

We need to know which of your cameras can carry detection before anything is shipped. That is the two-business-day assessment, and it is free.

02
Network access and a place to put it

The appliance needs power, a network path to the cameras, and somewhere to sit. Outbound connections only — no inbound ports, no public IP.

03
Someone on your side

A person who knows the site and can say which areas matter and when. A trial with nobody to tell us what counts as an incident will not tell you much.

04
Honest limits

If your cameras cannot see what you want detected, a trial will show that rather than fix it. We would rather tell you at the assessment than after 30 days.

At the End

Two outcomes, both of them fine

You continue

The appliance stays and moves onto the normal per-camera subscription at the tier your site actually needs. Nothing is billed for the trial period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to buy the appliance to try it?

No. The appliance is lent at no cost for the 30 days. If you do not continue, we arrange collection — there is no purchase, deposit or restocking charge for the trial itself.

What happens at the end of the 30 days?

One of two things. You continue, and the appliance stays and moves onto the normal per-camera subscription; or you do not, and we collect it. There is no automatic rollover onto a paid plan, and nothing starts billing unless you say so.

Does our data leave the site during the trial?

No continuous video leaves your premises during the trial, exactly as in a full deployment. Analytics run locally on the appliance. Alerts and short clips reach the people you nominate, over outbound connections only.

What happens to the data if we do not continue?

It is on your appliance, on your premises. When the appliance is collected the data on it is destroyed, and we can confirm that in writing if you need it for an internal sign-off.

Can we trial on part of the site?

Yes, and on a large estate it is usually the sensible route — run detection where incidents actually happen, judge it there, then extend. The assessment establishes which area is the useful test.

Do we need new cameras?

Usually not. The assessment confirms which existing cameras qualify and says plainly which do not. Where a camera genuinely cannot see what you need, we say so before the trial rather than letting it fail quietly during it.

How soon can it start?

The assessment comes back within two business days. Scheduling the install depends on your site and ours — tell us your timeline at contact@parvek.ai and we will be straight about what is achievable.
