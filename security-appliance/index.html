What It Does

Playback & evidence clips

Capabilities

What runs on it

The detection capabilities the appliance can run, grouped by what they are for. Which ones are enabled is a per-site decision made during the assessment.

Security

Who is on site, where they are, and whether they should be there.
Intruder / Unknown Person Detection +
Detects
A person whose face is not on your enrolled list of family, staff or contractors.
Where it helps
Perimeter, entrances, driveways, yards, store rooms and staff-only floors.
Example
Someone climbs the boundary wall at 2:50 AM and walks toward the house.
Configurable response
Mobile alert with snapshot and clip · voice warning · floodlight · siren where installed.
Known-Person Recognition +
Detects
Enrolled family members, staff and contractors, so routine movement does not raise alarms.
Where it helps
Main gate, reception, lobby and entry cameras.
Example
Your housekeeper arrives at 9 AM and is recognized instead of flagged as an intruder.
Configurable response
Silent log entry · named notification where you want one.
Unauthorized Access +
Detects
A known person in a place or at a time they are not permitted.
Where it helps
Cash offices, server and records rooms, pharmacy and inventory stores.
Example
An enrolled employee enters the stores at 11 PM, outside their permitted hours.
Configurable response
Named mobile alert with camera and clip · evidence retained.
Restricted-Zone Monitoring +
Detects
Any person inside a marked area during the hours it is off limits.
Where it helps
Plant rooms, racks, cabinets, dispatch bays and any area drawn as a zone.
Example
A person stands at the stock racks after the permitted window has closed.
Configurable response
Local voice warning in that area · light · silent alert and clip to the desk.
Banned Person / Watchlist +
Detects
A likely match against a watchlist you maintain on your own appliance.
Where it helps
Main gate, reception and visitor entrances.
Example
A dismissed employee or barred contractor returns to the site.
Configurable response
Alert with the matched photo so the desk confirms before acting.
Perimeter Intrusion +
Detects
A person crossing a boundary line or present in an area where nobody should be.
Where it helps
Walls, rear gates, parking, service entries and open yards.
Example
Movement along the rear boundary after dark, away from any path.
Configurable response
Voice warning · floodlight · siren where the site permits · alert with clip.
Loitering +
Detects
A person lingering in one area for longer than the time you set.
Where it helps
Gates, racks, parking, ATMs, entrances and quiet corridors.
Example
Someone waits beside the boundary for several minutes without approaching the gate.
Configurable response
Escalating alert · voice warning · evidence clip.
SOS / Panic Alert +
Detects
Not a detection — a person raises the alarm themselves, from the mobile app or the console.
Where it helps
Reception, cash offices, wards, the security desk, or simply from a phone in a pocket.
Example
A staff member at reception is threatened and needs help without announcing it out loud.
Configurable response
Immediate alert to everyone on the list — people on site and remote recipients — with the site and time.
Camera Tamper +
Detects
A camera deliberately blocked, covered, sprayed or turned away from what it was watching.
Where it helps
Every camera, but it matters most on gates, stores and cash areas.
Example
A camera view goes black shortly before an attempt on the premises.
Configurable response
Immediate critical alert · warning sound and light · clip retained.
Camera Health +
Detects
A camera that has dropped off the network, lost frame rate or stopped delivering usable images.
Where it helps
Across every camera on the system, continuously.
Example
A camera quietly fails on a Friday and nobody notices until footage is needed.
Configurable response
Health status per camera on the console · alert when a camera degrades or disconnects.

Safety

A visual layer that watches for what hurts people, alongside your existing systems.
Fire Detection +
Detects
Visible flame in the camera view.
Where it helps
Plant rooms, stores, kitchens, parking, high ceilings and open yards where heat takes minutes to reach a detector.
Example
A fire starts in a store room overnight with nobody on that floor.
Configurable response
Critical alert with clip · maintenance notified · hooter where configured.
Smoke Detection +
Detects
Visible smoke and haze building in the view.
Where it helps
Plant rooms, electrical panels, DG rooms, stores and parking.
Example
Smoke gathers near an electrical panel before any flame appears.
Configurable response
Critical alert with clip · maintenance notified.

Where it helps

Crowd Detection +

Where it helps
Gates, lobbies, canteens, OPD waiting areas and work bays.
Example
A gathering builds at the main gate during a shift change.
Configurable response
Alert with the live count and clip before it becomes an incident.
Machine Operator Missing +
Detects
A machine running with nobody at the operating position.
Where it helps
Shop floors, production lines, presses, packing machines and any station that should not run unattended.
Example
An operator steps away mid-cycle and the machine keeps running with nobody watching it.
Configurable response
Supervisor alert with clip · voice reminder in the area where configured.

Whether a designated post is occupied or empty.
Where it helps

Example

Configurable response

How many people are in a zone over time.
Where it helps
Waiting areas, work bays, canteens and meeting spaces.
Example
A waiting area regularly exceeds comfortable capacity at the same hour.
Configurable response
Occupancy trend report per zone and shift.
Reports +
Detects
Every event the appliance records, kept on site.
Where it helps
Across all cameras and capabilities in use.
Example
A month-end review of attendance, patrols, counting and compliance.
Configurable response
Compliance, attendance, counting and operational records from the console.

Physical Response

Detection is only the first step

An alert on a phone is not much use at 3 AM if nothing happens on site. PARVEK can trigger the response the premises allows — a voice warning in that area, the light, the siren — and keep the evidence either way.

  1. AI detects On the appliance
    →
  2. Voice warning In that area
    →
  3. Floodlight / siren Where permitted
    →
  4. Mobile alert With the frame
    →
  5. Evidence Clip kept on site

Speaker / Voice Warning

A local warning in the area that was breached — challenging the person where they are, not across the whole site.

Siren / Hooter

Audible escalation where the site permits it, linked to confirmed events rather than every movement.

Floodlight / Light

The area lights up as configured, deterring the person and giving the camera a usable image.

Mobile Alert

A notification to the security desk, owner or management with the frame that triggered it.

Evidence Clip

The video around the event, kept on the appliance for verification and follow-up.

Reports

Compliance, attendance, counting and operational records available from the console.

Response devices are installed and configured per site. Sirens and floodlights are used only where the premises permits them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I still need a separate NVR?

No. The appliance provides the recording and playback function alongside the AI and management, which is why the architecture shows cameras going straight to PARVEK.

How many cameras does it support?

That depends on the configuration — camera count, resolution, retention and which capabilities are running all affect it. We specify the appliance for your site after the assessment rather than quoting a universal number.

How much storage do I need?

It is a function of camera count, resolution, frame rate, whether recording is continuous or event-based, and the retention period. Recording usually runs on the sub-stream, which changes the arithmetic considerably. We size it during design.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Recording and detection keep running — they are local. Remote mobile alerts need connectivity to leave the site, but nothing on the premises stops working.

Is there a monthly subscription?

There is no mandatory cloud subscription for the AI to run. Ongoing costs are the service plan you choose.

Can we access it from a phone?

Yes — a mobile app and a web console, with users and roles controlling who sees which cameras and alerts.
