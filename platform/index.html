The Chain

Seven stages, one appliance

  1. 01

    Camera ingestion

    Works with supported existing camera infrastructure. IP and RTSP streams come onto the appliance from whatever mix of brands and generations the site already has.

  2. 02

    Edge AI

    Video is processed locally on the PARVEK appliance. Sensitive footage and real-time analysis stay on site, which also removes the dependence on a stable uplink for detection to work.

  3. 03

    Identity & tracking

    Observations are connected across time and scenes, so a person moving through several cameras is one subject rather than a series of unrelated sightings.

  4. 04

    Event engine

    Detections become meaningful security events by applying your rules — which area, during which hours, and who is permitted there.

  5. 05

    Alert manager

    Events are delivered as actionable notifications with the evidence attached, and can be acknowledged, resolved or escalated from the console or the app.

  6. 06

    Response integrations

    Speakers, sirens, lights and guard workflows are connected to the events that warrant them, so the site can respond rather than only report.

  7. 07

    Reporting

    Event evidence and operational knowledge are preserved on site — frames, clips, context and reports available for review and audit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PARVEK replace our recorder?

The appliance provides recording and playback alongside the AI, alerts and management, so a separate NVR is not required. Many sites run both during a transition and retire the old recorder once retention on the appliance covers their requirement.

What happens if our internet connection drops?

Detection, recording and local response keep running, because they are on the appliance. Connectivity is used to deliver remote notifications and, where you permit it, remote support.

How many cameras does one appliance support?

It depends on the configuration — camera count, resolution, retention and which capabilities are running all affect it. The appliance is specified for your site after the assessment rather than quoted as a universal number.

Can PARVEK work alongside our existing security systems?

Response devices are driven through relays and standard integrations, and events can be routed to the people and workflows you already use. Where you have an incumbent integrator maintaining cameras and cabling, we work alongside them.

AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.
