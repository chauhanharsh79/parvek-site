Financial Services

Evidence for every entry to a controlled room.

A branch network is many small sites with the same handful of controlled spaces in each: the cash office, the secure room, the back entrance. The question is always the same — who went in, when, and can you show it.

Start a Site Assessment See the event sources
The Environment

What makes this estate hard to watch

Many sites, few cameras each

A network of small estates rather than one large one, which changes both the deployment and the cost shape.

The controlled space is small and well defined

Cash office, secure room, service door. That makes configuration tight and event volume naturally low.

Evidence has to survive scrutiny

Audit, dispute and investigation all need the footage attached to the event rather than found later on a timeline.

Scenarios

From the trigger to the evidence

Which of these apply to you, and which cameras can carry them, is what the site survey establishes before anything is proposed.

01

Entry to the cash office

Trigger

A badge opens the cash office, in or out of hours.

Camera context

The camera covering the door captures the frame at the read.

Operator review

Whether the person matches the credential on record.

What happens next

Logged with the frame and the clip, retained for the period your policy sets.

02

After-hours presence in the branch

Trigger

A person detected inside a marked area when the branch is closed.

Camera context

The detection frame with the area drawn on it.

Operator review

Cleaner, engineer, or unexplained.

What happens next

Alert to the on-call manager with the clip attached.

03

Activity at the rear or service entrance

Trigger

Movement at a back door outside permitted hours.

Camera context

The doorway camera at the moment of detection.

Operator review

A judgement made from the picture, in seconds.

What happens next

Escalation to the nominated responders, evidence retained.

04

A branch camera goes dark

Trigger

A camera is obscured, moved or stops streaming.

Camera context

The last usable frame and the time it stopped.

Operator review

Raised centrally rather than noticed on the next visit.

What happens next

A fix scheduled before an incident finds the gap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this scale across a hundred branches?

Each branch runs its own appliance and processes locally, with events and roles rolled up centrally. The survey works out the per-branch shape first, because a network multiplies both the cost and any configuration mistake.

Does PARVEK control access to the cash office?

No. It reads door events and pairs them with footage. Locking and unlocking stays entirely with your access-control system.

What about the public area of the branch?

That is usually left out of configuration. The value concentrates on the controlled rooms and the out-of-hours window, where event volume is low and evidence matters most.

Can we retain evidence longer for disputes?

Retention is a site decision, bounded by storage on the appliance. It is sized during the assessment against the period your policy requires.
