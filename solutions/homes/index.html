Homes & Residential

AI Video Security for Homes & Residential Communities

Most homes and communities already have cameras. What they lack is anything watching them at 3 AM. PARVEK adds detection to the cameras already installed and lets the property respond on its own — a voice warning, a light, an alert with the frame attached.

What It Watches For

Built around how this site actually fails

Person detection at the perimeter

Someone on the boundary wall, in the driveway or at the rear gate, flagged the moment they appear rather than found on footage afterwards.

Known faces recognized

Family, staff and regular visitors enrolled, so routine arrivals do not fill your phone with alerts you learn to ignore.

Restricted areas by time

A terrace, store room, rear lane or boundary marked as an area, with the hours it should be empty.

Alerts with the evidence

A notification with the frame that triggered it and the clip around it — enough to tell a cat from a person before getting out of bed.

Response on the property

Voice warning, floodlight and siren where those are installed, triggered by the event rather than by someone watching.

Community gates and parking

For residential communities: gate movement, visitor entries and parking areas, with a record the committee can review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it alert on every passing animal?

The system is tuned to people, and thresholds, areas and timings are adjusted on your own footage during commissioning. The goal is alerts you trust rather than alerts you mute.

Can it use the cameras I already have?

Usually, if they are IP or RTSP cameras in serviceable condition. We check compatibility free and tell you plainly which views cannot support reliable detection.

What happens at 3 AM when nobody is awake?

Beyond the notification, the property can act: a voice warning from the speaker in that area, the floodlight switching on, and a siren where it is installed and permitted.

Does my footage go to a cloud service?

Video and AI processing can run locally on the appliance in your home. Connectivity is used to deliver alerts to your phone and, if you want it, remote support.
