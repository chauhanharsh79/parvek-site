AI Video Security for Critical & High-Security Environments

Some sites cannot send video off the premises, and cannot wait for someone to review footage afterwards. PARVEK processes locally, raises events in real time, drives the response on site, and keeps an auditable record of what happened and who acted on it.

What It Watches For

Built around how this site actually fails

Processing stays on site

Video and AI run on the appliance inside the perimeter. There is no requirement to send footage to a third-party cloud for detection to work.

Layered restricted areas

Areas within areas, each with its own permitted hours and named list — so a breach of the outer fence and a breach of the inner room are different events.

Identity-aware events

Who an event involves, not only that it happened, with watchlists held on your own appliance.

Response without a human in the loop

Voice warning, light and siren triggered by the rule, so the site responds in the seconds before anyone can.

Auditable record

Every event, every action taken on it and every user, recorded — for review, investigation and audit.

Operates without connectivity

Detection, recording and local response continue when the uplink is down, by design rather than as a degraded mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the system run fully offline?

Detection, recording and local response are on the appliance and keep working without connectivity. Remote notification requires a network path, but nothing on site depends on one.

What audit trail exists?

Events, the evidence behind them, and the actions taken — acknowledged, resolved, escalated, and by whom. Users and roles determine who can see and do what.

How do you handle sensitive footage?

It stays on the appliance inside your premises, under your retention policy. We do not require a copy, and remote support access is only where you grant it.

Can you support our existing procedures?

Response rules, escalation paths and who gets notified are configured to your procedures rather than imposed. Where you have an incumbent integrator maintaining the estate, we work alongside them.
