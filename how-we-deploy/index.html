How We Deploy

From camera list to a site that responds

Every deployment follows the same sequence, whether it is eight cameras in one building or eighty across a plant. The parts that matter most are the two nobody advertises: honest compatibility, and tuning on your own footage.

Start a Site Assessment I already have cameras
The Sequence

Eleven steps, in order

  1. 01

    Site Assessment

    We walk the facility with your team and map risks, coverage, blind spots, lighting and the cameras and network already in place.

  2. 02

    Camera Compatibility

    Which existing cameras can carry detection, at what resolution and frame rate, and which locations need something different.

  3. 03

    Coverage & Zone Design

    Camera positions, the zones that need rules, permitted hours, the people to enroll and where response devices belong.

  4. 04

    Infrastructure Work

    Only where it is needed: additional cameras, cabling, PoE capacity, network segmentation, power protection and mounting.

  5. 05

    Appliance Installation

    The PARVEK appliance is installed on site and every camera is brought onto it, with recording and retention configured.

  6. 06

    AI Configuration

    Zones, rules, permitted hours, enrolled people, schedules and the capabilities your facility actually needs — set on your own camera views.

  7. 07

    Response Integration

    Speakers, horns, lights and floodlights wired and linked to the right events, where your site permits them.

  8. 08

    Tuning on Your Footage

    Thresholds adjusted against what your cameras actually see, over real days and nights, rather than assumed from a datasheet.

  9. 09

    Testing & Commissioning

    Cameras, recording, AI, alerts and every configured response tested end to end, with you present, before handover.

  10. 10

    Training & Handover

    Your security and admin users trained on the console, the mobile app and what each alert means — including how to mark a false one.

  11. 11

    Service & Support

    Ongoing health checks, issue resolution, software updates and maintenance under the service plan you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deployment take?

Where compatible cameras and cabling are already in place, a site can be running detection quickly — the work is assessment, appliance, configuration and tuning. A build that needs new cameras, cabling and response devices is phased around your operations and scheduled at design stage.

Do you quote before or after the assessment?

After. The assessment establishes camera compatibility, what infrastructure is needed, storage and retention, and which capabilities are worth enabling. Quoting before that means guessing, and the guess is always wrong in one direction.

Can we pilot it on a few cameras first?

Yes, and it is often the sensible route on a large site: pick the area where incidents actually happen, run detection there, and judge it on your own footage before extending. It also surfaces lighting and camera-placement issues while they are cheap to fix.

Who configures the rules?

We do, with your team, during configuration and commissioning — zones marked on the actual camera views, permitted hours set to your shifts, and the people who need enrolling enrolled. You are trained to adjust them afterwards.

Can we keep our existing integrator?

Yes. Where you have an incumbent doing cabling and camera maintenance, we work alongside them — we need network access to the cameras and a home for the appliance, not ownership of your estate.
