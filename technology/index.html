Design Principles

Six things the architecture commits to

Local AI processing

Detection runs on the appliance inside your premises. Sensitive video and real-time analysis stay on site, and detection does not stop when the uplink does.

Existing-camera compatibility

Supported IP and RTSP cameras are brought onto the appliance whatever the brand or generation, so AI can be added without replacing an entire estate.

Real-time event detection

Events are raised while they are still happening, which is what makes a response — a voice warning, a light, a call — worth triggering at all.

Evidence-linked alerts

Every event carries the frame, clip, time and context behind it, so an alert can be verified rather than merely believed.

Configurable response workflows

Which events trigger which devices, and who gets told, is set per area and per site rather than fixed in the product.

Designed for on-site security operations

Built around a security desk that has to act on what it sees: an event queue, roles, camera health and an auditable record.

PARVEK Appliance

One appliance. NVR + AI + management.

Camera streams, recording, playback, AI events, evidence clips, alerts, reports, users and roles and camera health — centralised in one appliance inside your premises. Faces, clips and reports stay on site.

What We Don’t Claim

Where the limits are

Security buyers are sold certainty constantly. These are the parts of the picture that get left out, stated up front instead.

Accuracy depends on the camera

Lighting, angle, distance and lens decide what any model can detect. Where a location cannot support a capability reliably, we say so during the assessment rather than enabling it and hoping.

Tuned on your footage

Thresholds are adjusted against what your cameras actually see, across real days and nights, rather than assumed from a benchmark.

No guarantees of completeness

AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does our video leave the site?

Video and AI processing can run locally on the appliance, keeping footage and real-time analysis on the premises. Connectivity is used to deliver remote notifications and, where you permit it, remote support.

Who can see what?

Users and roles control which cameras, alerts and reports each person can access, and actions on events are recorded, so there is an auditable trail of who did what.

What about biometric and privacy regulation?

Requirements vary by jurisdiction — several US states have specific biometric statutes, and notice or consent obligations may apply to staff. Keeping enrolled faces and clips on your own appliance helps, but you should confirm your obligations with counsel. The system is useful with recognition disabled, using areas, timing and person detection alone.

How are updates handled?

Improvements to detection and the console are applied to your appliance as part of the service arrangement, on a schedule you agree rather than pushed without notice.

AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.
