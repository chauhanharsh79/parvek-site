Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to replace our cameras?

Usually not. PARVEK works with supported existing camera infrastructure, so organizations can add AI video security without replacing every camera already installed. We confirm which of your cameras can carry detection during the site assessment, and say plainly which ones cannot.

Where does the video and the AI processing run?

Video and AI processing can run locally on the PARVEK appliance, keeping sensitive video and real-time analysis on site and reducing dependence on cloud connectivity.

What does PARVEK actually do with what it sees?

It detects people, vehicles, faces, events and activity in real time, then turns those observations into alerts, evidence, reports and actions — including triggering speakers, sirens and lights where those are installed.

How many capabilities will we use?

There are 18, grouped into Detect, Understand and Act, and no site runs all of them. Which ones are enabled is decided during the site assessment and configured at commissioning.

What is the relationship to Parvekshak?

PARVEK is the shorter global identity for the same platform, which began as Parvekshak and has been deployed on customer sites since 2025. PARVEKSHAK remains the brand in India at parvekshak.com.

AI surveillance and analytics are technology-assisted and do not guarantee prevention or detection of every security incident or 100% accuracy.
