Physical Response

Detection is only the first step

An alert on a phone is not much use at 3 AM if nothing happens on site. PARVEK can trigger the response the premises allows — a voice warning in that area, the light, the siren — and keep the evidence either way.

  1. AI detects On the appliance
    →
  2. Voice warning In that area
    →
  3. Floodlight / siren Where permitted
    →
  4. Mobile alert With the frame
    →
  5. Evidence Clip kept on site

Speaker / Voice Warning

A local warning in the area that was breached — challenging the person where they are, not across the whole site.

Siren / Hooter

Audible escalation where the site permits it, linked to confirmed events rather than every movement.

Floodlight / Light

The area lights up as configured, deterring the person and giving the camera a usable image.

Mobile Alert

A notification to the security desk, owner or management with the frame that triggered it.

Evidence Clip

The video around the event, kept on the appliance for verification and follow-up.

Reports

Compliance, attendance, counting and operational records available from the console.

Response devices are installed and configured per site. Sirens and floodlights are used only where the premises permits them.

Restricted Zones & Access

A camera records the room. A zone watches it.

Your store rooms, server or records room, plant rooms and entrances are already on camera. What is missing is a rule: which area, during which hours, and who is allowed in it. PARVEK marks each one as a zone and alerts the security desk the moment it is broken — not three weeks later when someone reviews the footage.

Rule part 01 Area

A polygon drawn on the camera view — the racks or the cabinet, not the whole frame.

+
Rule part 02 Permitted hours

Working hours, night only, or always.

+
Rule part 03 Who is allowed

Enrolled staff only, a named list, or nobody after hours.

=
Result Named alert

Security desk, in seconds, with the clip — and the area can answer back with voice and light.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PARVEK replace our existing cameras?

Usually not. Compatible IP and RTSP cameras in serviceable condition connect straight to the PARVEK appliance and gain AI detection, alerts, recording and reporting. We assess what you have and tell you plainly which cameras are worth keeping and which cannot deliver the coverage a location needs.

Where does our video go?

Nowhere. Detection and recording run on the appliance inside your facility. Faces, clips and reports stay on site, and there is no mandatory cloud subscription for the AI to run. An internet connection is used only to deliver remote alerts and remote support if you want them.

Is this a separate system from our NVR?

The PARVEK appliance provides the recording and playback function alongside the AI, alerts and management, so a separate NVR is not required. Camera count, retention and storage are specified per configuration.

What happens after a detection?

The event raises an alert with the frame and an evidence clip, and can trigger the response configured for that area — a local voice warning, a light or floodlight, and a siren or horn where the site permits it. That is the difference between a system that reports and one that acts.

How many capabilities will we actually use?

There are 24, and no site runs all of them. Capabilities are selected during the assessment and configured at commissioning — a warehouse may run perimeter, restricted zones, box counting and attendance; a hospital may run watchlists, staff-only rooms, fall detection and crowding.

Who is behind PARVEK?

PARVEK is the US brand for the Parvekshak platform, which has been running on customer sites since 2025. The product, the appliance and the detection models are the same; the name is shorter for this market.
