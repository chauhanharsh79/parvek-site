Cameras

Right camera for every location

We are not tied to one camera type. During the assessment we select the camera, lens, mounting position and power/network method for each location — and reuse compatible cameras you already own where that is the practical choice. Everything then connects to the PARVEK Security Appliance for recording, AI, alerts, playback and reports.

Fixed IP Cameras

Where
Entrances, corridors, rooms, shop floors and perimeter runs.
Why
Everyday coverage of a known, fixed field of view — the backbone of most deployments.
Connects
PoE over a single cable into the network switch and on to the appliance.

PTZ Cameras

Where
Large yards, campuses, parking areas and open plant land.
Why
One camera covers a wide area, with pan, tilt and zoom for detail on demand.
Connects
PoE or dedicated power, streaming to the appliance over the same network.

Varifocal Cameras

Where
Locations where the exact angle and reach must be set on site.
Why
The focal length is adjusted during installation for precise coverage of a gate, aisle or approach.
Connects
PoE into the switch, tuned at commissioning and left fixed.

Wi-Fi Cameras

Where
Spots where running Ethernet is impractical — retrofits, rented premises, outbuildings.
Why
Coverage without new cabling, where a wireless link is appropriate and stable.
Connects
Over your Wi-Fi network to the appliance; local power at the camera.

Low-Light / Night Cameras

Where
Perimeters, driveways, yards and any area with difficult lighting.
Why
Usable images in low light, which is when most intrusion detection matters.
Connects
PoE into the switch, with lighting and lux checked during the assessment.

Specialized Cameras

Where
Gates and parking where vehicle or license-plate applications are required.
Why
Specialized requirements such as license-plate recognition, subject to compatibility and project scope.
Connects
Into the same network and appliance, scoped per project.
Infrastructure

What holds the system up

Most camera systems fail at the cabling, the switch or the power long before the camera gives out. This is the part nobody photographs and everybody feels.

PoE Switches

Power and data over one cable to each camera, sized for the camera count and future expansion.

Ethernet Cabling

Routed through conduit, labeled and terminated properly so service in year three takes minutes.

Networking

A segregated, stable network for the security system rather than sharing whatever is already busy.

Storage

Sized for the camera count and the retention period you actually need, on the appliance.

UPS & Power Protection

So the system survives the cuts and spikes that come with the territory.

Mounts & Weatherproofing

Brackets, junction boxes and glanding that hold up outdoors for years, not months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply every camera type on every project?

No. The point of the portfolio is selection: we pick the camera, lens, mounting position and power/network method that suits each location. Most sites use two or three types, not six.

Can I supply my own cameras?

Yes. We install customer-supplied cameras and integrate compatible existing ones. We will say plainly if a camera cannot deliver the coverage or the detection quality a location needs.

Is license-plate recognition available?

Vehicle and license-plate applications are handled per project, subject to camera compatibility, placement, lighting and scope. It is scoped during the assessment rather than assumed.

What resolution do you install?

It depends on the distance to the subject and what the camera needs to establish — presence, identification, or a plate. That calculation happens during the coverage design, and it is the reason two cameras on the same wall may be different models.

Not sure which cameras your site needs?

That is what the assessment answers. We walk the premises, work out what each location has to see, and specify the cameras and infrastructure to match.
